# The instances, their VNIC attachments and the shapes, in the compartment of the nodes.

# The boot volume a launch creates and a terminate deletes.
Allow group <group> to use volume-family in compartment <compartment>
# The subnet and the network security groups of a launch, and the VNICs' addresses
# (also in the compartments of brought resources).
Allow group <group> to use virtual-network-family in compartment <compartment>
# The region subscriptions and the availability domains.
Allow group <group> to inspect tenancies in tenancy
Allow group <group> to inspect compartments in tenancy
# The quotas are read on the root compartment (the tenancy), for the whole account's usage.
Allow group <group> to inspect resource-availability in tenancy
Allow group <group> to inspect limits in tenancy
